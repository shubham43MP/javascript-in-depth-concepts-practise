## One final suggestion

Given your **9 years of React/TypeScript experience**, don't let DSA consume all your preparation. A balanced split like this is likely to maximize your chances:

* **50%** DSA
* **30%** Frontend system design (component architecture, rendering, caching, performance, accessibility)
* **20%** JavaScript, TypeScript, browser internals, and behavioral interview preparation

For senior frontend roles, clearing the coding round gets you to the next stage, but your frontend expertise and system design discussions are often what determine whether you ultimately receive the offer.
