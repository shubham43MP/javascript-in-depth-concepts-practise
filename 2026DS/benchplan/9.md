this in JavaScript is generally determined by the call site rather than where a function is declared. For normal functions, I think about four binding rules: default binding, implicit binding through obj.method(), explicit binding through call, apply, or bind, and constructor binding through new. Arrow functions are different because they don't create their own this; they lexically capture this from the enclosing scope. This distinction becomes especially important when passing methods as callbacks, where implicit binding can be lost
