Weeks 1–2: Rebuild fundamentals

Focus on:

Arrays
Strings
Hash Maps
Two Pointers
Sliding Window
Prefix Sum

Target: 35–40 problems

Don't move on until you can identify the pattern within a few minutes of reading a problem.

Week 3: Linked Lists, Stacks, Queues

Focus on:

Linked Lists
Fast & Slow pointers
Stacks
Monotonic Stack (basics)
Queues
Deques

Target: 20–25 problems

Week 4: Trees

Focus on:

DFS
BFS
Binary Trees
BST
Recursion
Lowest Common Ancestor

Target: 25–30 problems

Trees are asked very frequently.

Week 5: Graphs

Focus on:

BFS
DFS
Topological Sort
Union-Find (basic)
Shortest Path (understand, don't obsess)

Target: 20 problems

Week 6: Binary Search, Heaps, Intervals

These topics appear regularly in interviews.

Target: 25 problems

Week 7: Dynamic Programming

Don't try to master every DP problem.

Learn the common patterns:

Fibonacci-style
House Robber
Knapsack
Grid DP
Longest Increasing Subsequence
Longest Common Subsequence

Target: 20 problems

Week 8: Mock interviews

Each day:

Solve 2 medium problems in 35–40 minutes each.
Explain your approach out loud.
Review mistakes and identify recurring weaknesses.
Total problem count

Aim for roughly:

120–150 carefully selected problems

Not:

500 random problems

Quality matters much more than quantity.

A simple progression

For each topic:

Solve 3–5 Easy problems to understand the pattern.
Solve 12–15 Medium problems until you can recognize it quickly.
Solve 2–3 Hard problems to stretch yourself.

Then move on.
